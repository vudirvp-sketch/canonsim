**What it is.** A generator library for cultures and institutions under
pressure: compact mechanisms that turn constraint, scarcity, residue, and
knowledge asymmetry into social structure — mined as MECHANISMS, never
imported as canon.

**Concrete mechanics.** The eight transferable mechanisms, each with its
chain form:

1. **Prohibition as causal generator.** A hard restriction forces
   compensating adaptations in economy, ritual, naming, humour, and
   institutions. Invariant: constraint propagation — removing one
   affordance forces a family of replacements elsewhere.
2. **Negative space as function removal.**
   `REMOVED FUNCTION → REQUIRED REPLACEMENT → NEW CARRIER / INSTITUTION → NEW COST → NEW FAILURE MODE → NEW ORDINARY BEHAVIOR`.
   Stronger than "why does this society lack X": it asks what function
   must still be performed and what carries it now — a causal
   diagnostic, not a descriptive trait.
3. **Residue as lifecycle.**
   `EVENT → RESIDUE → CARRIER → HOLDER → TRANSFORMATION → TRANSFER → CLEARANCE / INHERITANCE → NEW OPTIONS`.
   A consequence remains actionable because it has a carrier (debt,
   reputation, name, obligation, inherited status, object, institutional
   claim, physical trace), a holder, and a clearance path.
4. **Meso institutions as coupling nodes.**
   `PRESSURE → RESOURCE / OBLIGATION → MESO UNIT → INTERNAL ROLES → DECISION → EVENT → DISTRIBUTED KNOWLEDGE → RESIDUE → FUTURE OPTIONS`.
   A meso institution is valuable when it converts a recurring pressure
   into a durable rule of interaction — more than a named location or
   faction subunit.
5. **Heterogeneous meso agency.** An aggregate actor need not have one
   goal, one knowledge state, or one response; subgroups with different
   roles, stakes, and maps produce partial coordination and different
   local consequences.
6. **Social carriers.**
   `CARRIER → PUBLIC RECOGNITION → CURRENT HOLDER → ATTACHED RIGHT / DUTY / MEMORY → TRANSFER → RELATIONSHIP CHANGE`.
   A persistent carrier (object, right, name, obligation) transports
   accumulated social state across actor and time boundaries —
   connecting household ↔ workshop ↔ guild ↔ market without a new
   subsystem.
7. **Humor generator.**
   `PROHIBITION + SCARCITY + RESIDUE → LOCAL CONTRADICTION → CULTURE-SPECIFIC JOKE FORM → SOCIAL SIGNAL / COST`.
   Humor is generated by constraints, not temperament; the taboo map is
   cultural structure; form follows the interaction regime (short or
   accumulative, immediate or deferred, literal or allusive, public or
   private); the cheapest safe joke and the most dangerous joke are
   complementary probes.
8. **Natural-pattern transfer.** Biological patterns (eusociality,
   mycorrhizal exchange, parasite-host, cleaner fish, necrophagy,
   succession, mimicry) donate interaction mechanisms translated into
   social constraints — never races, organisms, or lore.

**What we take.** The four W4 research operators — the function-loss
probe, the residue lifecycle trace, the meso assembly probe, the
biography crisis probe (`WORLD_AUTHORING.md` §5/§7/§8, `WORLD_TESTS.md`
§3) — and the humor generator (`WORLD_TESTS.md` §3), each with its
success criterion (D-189). The third-order synthesis: remove/restrict an
affordance → the society must preserve a function → a replacement carrier
or meso institution emerges → the carrier accumulates residue → residue
moves through heterogeneous actors → knowledge asymmetry changes its
meaning → the future option space changes (`WORLD_AUTHORING.md` §19).

**What we adapt.** The carrier mechanism as ONE bounded W4 experiment on
the crossing household (the punt pole, the waybill, or the toll record),
riding existing primitives only — items, events, relations, knowledge
(`WORLD_WORKPLAN.md` §6). The two external donor mechanisms named by the
corpus — the irrigation-tribunal practitioner water-governance shape and
the constructed-kinship (milk-kinship) edge — mechanism-only, each
carrying a named falsifier before any adoption (the function-loss probe /
the compression test), never the institutions themselves.

**What inspires us.** "Mine the generator library, not the
worldbuilding layer": Kurvitz's value for this repo is the
operationalization of principles the worldbuild surface already owns —
the doctrine was present; the generators and probes were not.

**Strengths.** Every mechanism arrives compact, chained, and with its
failure mode; the probe forms are self-falsifying (a failed probe names
the missing substrate instead of hiding it); the generators compose with
each other (the synthesis chain) and with the existing doctrine
(`AFFORDANCE → CONSTRAINT → COST → CONSEQUENCE → INTERPRETATION`,
residue-first authoring, the meso bridge).

**Weaknesses.** Source-form pressure: the corpus ships ready-made races,
phonotactic catalogues, humor tables, life-goal tables, and exotic
institutions that would import surface glamour as catalogue growth. Several
source claims are historically or theoretically contested (usable as
pressure generators or research questions, never as unqualified world
facts). The institution examples are strongest as donors of mechanism and
weakest as one-to-one transplant candidates.

**Verdict.** PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (intake-31, D-189): a generator library
for the W4/W5 world-track passes. The do-not-import list is binding: no
races or organism cultures, no full name catalogues, no humor tables, no
life-goal tables as normative structure, no exact source thresholds, no
ethnographic one-to-one replacements, no contested
linguistic-relativity claims as world law, no renamed source institutions
("potlatch"-family imports). Canonical adoption runs through the
worldbuild owners only (`WORLD_AUTHORING.md` / `WORLD_TESTS.md` /
`WORLD_WORKPLAN.md`), each adoption carrying its success criterion and
reversal condition.
