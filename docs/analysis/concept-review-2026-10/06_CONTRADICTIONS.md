## The high-value synthesis

The previous review often framed tensions as binary choices. The adopted design deliberately creates third options:

```text
full simulation vs aggressive LOD
→ obligation-preserving abstraction/refinement

strict serialism vs CRDT/MVCC
→ strict order + certified commutative cohorts

LLM planner vs trivial NPC actions
→ persistent commitments + bounded deterministic action selection

prose-only social language vs prose-as-fact
→ typed speech acts admitted through normal simulation

confidence score vs always-ask clarification
→ ambiguity collapse only when canonical effect surfaces are equivalent

locality by geography vs global scans
→ causal-demand locality + deferred semantic debt
```

These syntheses are preferred because they preserve the standing architecture rather than adding a second intelligence or truth layer.
