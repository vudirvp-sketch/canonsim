# Resonance

**Status:** active working canon.

This document merges the Resonance mechanism and its torture-test boundary
work. It owns the ontology and limitations of the phenomenon; civilization-
specific applications belong in the civilization owner.

## 1. Core model

### HARD

**Resonance is not energy.** It is a property of interaction between related
systems.

A useful working intuition is:

`RESonant leverage ~ COHERENCE × COMPATIBILITY × ANCHOR × SCALE / DISTANCE`

This is an ontological intuition, not a mandatory gameplay formula.

Resonance can make an already physically possible transition easier,
more stable, more reproducible, or more persistent. It cannot simply declare an
impossible state to be true.

## 2. Three observable modes

### Binding

Stabilizing a structure in or near a chosen state.

### Transfer

Moving load, influence, state, or structure between compatible systems.

### Echo

A system retains a structural trace of a previous state that can influence
future restoration or reconstruction.

These are three useful observed regimes of one phenomenon, not three separate
magic schools.

## 3. What creates a strong coupling

Potential strength increases with:

- internal coherence of the system;
- compatibility between the systems;
- quality of the anchor;
- manageable scale;
- useful proximity or shared structure.

An anchor may be a material, location, structure, device, organism, network,
ritual, or another stable carrier.

Distance includes more than meters. Informational isolation and structural
difference also weaken useful coupling.

## 4. Ten hard limits

### R1 — NO EX NIHILO

Resonance does not create matter or energy from nothing.

### R2 — NO NEW INFORMATION

It does not produce complex information that was never present in the coupled
systems.

### R3 — NO CAUSAL BYPASS

It cannot produce an effect without a physically meaningful causal route.

### R4 — NO FREE SCALE

A local effect does not automatically scale to a city, region or planet.

### R5 — NO FREE REMOTE COUPLING

Long-distance effects need specific structural, informational or infrastructural
support.

### R6 — NO PERFECT RESTORATION

Restoration is limited by what structure and information remain recoverable.

### R7 — NO PERFECT IDENTITY

Reconstructing a state does not prove continuity of subjective identity.

### R8 — STABILITY HAS DEBT

Holding an unusual state creates maintenance, stress, side effects, dependency,
or another accumulating burden.

### R9 — COMPLEXITY HAS A FAILURE SURFACE

More complex coupled systems expose more classes of failure and more possible
cascade paths.

### R10 — NO ORACLE

Resonance cannot know what reality itself does not contain.

## 5. Conservation view

The torture-test work converges on five preservation constraints:

| Conservation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Information | lost information cannot be conjured back perfectly |
| Energy | useful work has a source and a cost |
| Causality | effects retain a causal route |
| Identity | restoration does not automatically restore subjective continuity |
| Stability | long-lived artificial states accumulate maintenance/dependency |

These are the cleanest short-form explanation of why Resonance does not solve
every problem.

## 6. Cost is part of the phenomenon

Resonance costs do not have to be money. A capability may pay through:

- energy;
- materials;
- information or expertise;
- infrastructure;
- maintenance;
- side effects transferred elsewhere.

The cost should be diegetic and should create social or institutional
consequences when the capability matters.

## 7. Persistence and path dependence

### WORKING

Resonance can exhibit hysteresis-like behavior: systems that have remained in a
state for a long time may be easier to keep in or return to that state.

This creates a useful bridge between physics and history:

`past configuration → easier continuation → institutional lock-in → path dependence`.

This is one reason ancient infrastructure can remain extraordinarily effective
in its native environment while being difficult to reproduce elsewhere.

## 8. Why regional "magics" differ

Different environments can expose different Resonance regimes:

| Region type | Dominant regime | Main dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | biological persistence | living ecosystem + long time |
| Mountains | structural stability | materials + maintenance |
| Industry | repeatable amplification | energy + standardized infrastructure |
| Necropolis | information persistence | archives + incomplete Echo |
| Underwater | containment | continuous habitat integrity |
| Sky | mass/stability | extreme legacy infrastructure |
| Maritime / pirate | may use little Resonance | mobility, geography, information |

The shared foundation does not imply shared practice, terminology, or social
meaning.

## 9. Torture-test boundary

The source test suite supports these current dispositions:

| Phenomenon | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| Teleportation | limited, infrastructure-dependent gates |
| Resurrection | reconstruction may work; identity remains unresolved |
| Prophecy | prediction/sensitivity possible; not absolute knowledge |
| Mind reading | complex coupling only; not universal |
| Longevity | possible as a costly, supported process |
| Weather control | local/regional engineering, not arbitrary global command |
| Artificial life | possible; not effortless |
| Artificial minds / AI | possible; continuity and maintenance remain problems |
| FTL | possible as a late-stage advanced topology hypothesis, not current canon |
| Terraforming | possible only at very high systemic cost and lock-in |
| Time travel | rejected by current model |
| Universal mind control | rejected as a general effect |
| Free energy | rejected |
| Matter creation | rejected |
| Perfect resurrection | rejected |
| Omniscience | rejected |

## 10. Authoring rule for a new capability

Every new high-impact Resonance capability must be answerable as:

`INPUT → COMPATIBILITY → ANCHOR → EFFECT → SCALE → PRICE → FAILURE → MAINTENANCE → SOCIAL CONSEQUENCE`.

If the chain cannot be explained, the capability is not ready for active world
canon.

## 11. Meaning boundary

Resonance should change what civilizations can do and therefore what they must
organize around. It should not become the world's only content source.

The setting stays stronger when one major civilization can be defined by a
non-Resonance economic or geographic regime while another is deeply dependent
on it.
